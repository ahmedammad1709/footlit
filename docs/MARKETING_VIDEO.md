# Footlit launch video: the plan

One concept, cut three ways: a **60 s desktop hero**, a **35 s vertical** for Reels / Shorts / TikTok, and a **15 s teaser** for ads and stories. Everything below is written so that someone could shoot and edit it from this page alone.

---

## 1. The idea in one line

**"Your merges deserve better."** It opens on the plain, silent GitHub merge everyone knows, then detonates into six full productions, then shows exactly how it works, and ends on one calm line: free, private, installs in a minute.

Tone: a thriller trailer for something small and joyful. Dark, tense, fast, then a release of pressure. Brand colours do the work: ink black, tungsten amber, paper cream, one red.

## 2. My take on your flow (and two changes)

Your flow is right: **fast theme montage to build tension, then how it works in a real browser.** Keep it. Two adjustments that will make it hit harder:

1. **Open with a 3-second setup, not the montage.** Viewers decide in about 3 seconds. A montage that starts cold is just flashing images with no stakes. If the first thing they see is a perfectly ordinary GitHub merge that does *nothing*, the montage becomes the answer to a question they did not know they had. Tension needs a "before".
2. **Show the flow as one continuous story, not a feature list.** Instead of "here is theme 1, 2, 3", follow one developer's afternoon: create a repo, open a PR, break the build, fix it, merge. Each moment triggers a different show. The viewer learns how it works without being told.

Honest caveat for the vertical cut: **Footlit runs in desktop browsers only** (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Vertical is a social *format*, not a mobile app. Always show a desktop browser in frame and put "Chrome · Edge · Firefox, on desktop" in the last card, so nobody expects an Android/iPhone install.

## 7. How to capture the footage (real, not faked)

**Rule: everything in the demo is the real extension on real github.com.** That is the product's whole credibility.

Setup:
- A **fresh browser profile** with only Footlit installed. Hide the bookmarks bar, close other extensions, set a clean wallpaper.
- GitHub in **dark mode**, a **test account or a throwaway repo** (`acme-demo`). Hide notifications and personal repo names.
- Browser zoom 110% to 125% so text reads on a phone; window 1920x1080 exactly.
- Record at **60 fps** (OBS Studio, free). Enable a **cursor highlight** or click ripple.
- Do each flow 3 times and keep the best take.

Flows to record (each 20 to 40 s of raw footage):
1. Toolbar icon, open the popup, switch theme (one smooth motion).
2. Create a new repository, land on it (Terminal).
3. Open a pull request on a branch whose workflow **fails**, watch Mission Control abort.
4. Re-run with a fix: checks go green, then **merge** (Marquee).
5. Delete the test repo (curtains close), optional B-roll.
6. Settings page with the live preview dock, scroll slowly (B-roll for "A roomy settings page").

For the **montage clips** you can also screen-record the theme lab on the website at full screen, or use the extension's Rehearse button on a blank GitHub page. Record each theme at 1920x1080 and again at 1080x1920 for the vertical cut.

## 8. Words on screen (all kinetic type, League Gothic)

- Style: uppercase, tracking +2%, tungsten amber `#F2A81D` on ink `#1B1612`, paper `#F1E8D4` for secondary lines. Slam in on the beat, hold 0.4 s, cut.
- Never more than 6 words on screen at once. Never a full sentence unless it is the hook or the end card.
- Captions (for any voiceover) sit in the lower third, 2 lines max, high contrast, no background boxes.
- Keep the hook and CTA wording identical across the three cuts so they reinforce each other.

## 9. Sound and music

- **Original or licensed only.** Never use a commercial track; platforms mute or remove videos and strike accounts. Safe sources: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat (free tier with credit), or a paid licence (Artlist, Epidemic Sound) if you want a bigger sound.
- Search for: **"cinematic tension build 120 bpm"**, **"trailer riser drop"**, **"dark electronic pulse"**. You want a track with a clear build and a hard stop you can place at 0:20.
- Sound effects pack (free, from Pixabay/Freesound with licence checked): riser, impact, whoosh, confetti pop, camera shutter or glitch, keyboard clicks, soft bulb "ting".
- Mix: music -14 LUFS integrated, hits up to -8 LUFS, final export -14 LUFS for social (-16 for YouTube is also fine). No clipping.
- Leave **two beats of true silence** at 0:20. It will feel like a hole in the video, in the best way.

## 10. Editing recipe

Tools: **DaVinci Resolve** (free, best quality) or **CapCut** (fastest, templates, auto-captions). Whichever you pick, set the project to 1920x1080 @ 30 or 60 fps, then duplicate the timeline for the vertical.

- Cut **on the beat**: put a marker on every beat of the track first, then snap clips to the markers.
- **Punch-ins:** at the key moment of each theme, scale the clip 100% to 108% over 0.4 s.
- **Speed ramps** on the demo clips: normal speed through the click, 150% through the waiting, back to normal for the payoff.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only in the montage. Use one glitch/whip transition between the montage and the demo. No cross-dissolves; they weaken the energy.
- **Colour:** crush the blacks slightly, keep amber saturated, add a very light film grain (3%) and a subtle vignette. Do not use any purple-blue gradient look.
- **Captions:** burn them in. For auto-captions, review every word.
- **Export:** H.264, high bitrate (16 to 20 Mbps for 1080p), AAC 320 kbps.
