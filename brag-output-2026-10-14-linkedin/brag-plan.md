# Footlit: A merge deserves a moment

## Planning rubric
1. Footlit is a free Chrome and Firefox extension that turns GitHub events into short, silent celebrations.
2. The product's line is "You shipped it. Take a bow."
3. Hook: enormous editorial type over a tiny purple merged badge. The mismatch is the joke.
4. Show real Marquee, Terminal and Pixel Arcade animations, captured from the actual engine, plus the actual extension popup.
5. 24 seconds gives the main celebration room to resolve and the CTA five seconds to read.
6. Polished, playful theatre poster brought to life. Warm paper, tungsten, ink. No generic SaaS claims.
7. Original 120 BPM electronic score: a dry opening click, warm bass and syncopated plucks; no narration. Fully comprehensible muted.
8. "A merged PR deserves a little more than a purple badge. I built Footlit to give GitHub wins a moment of their own."
9. Merge a pull request → Marquee celebrates; choose a different theme → checks and releases get their own treatment.

## Format
1080 × 1350 (4:5), 30 fps, 24 seconds. Designed for a mobile LinkedIn feed.

## Storyboard
- 0–3: ALL THAT WORK. / ONE TINY BADGE. Purple merged badge, angular cursor arrival, a dry click. Headline stays readable. Warm paper.
- 3–9.5: GIVE YOUR MERGE / A MOMENT. Real engine footage occupies most of the frame. Merge confirmation at 3.7, celebration at 4.0. Brand strip: FOOTLIT / A little theatre for GitHub.
- 9.5–16: YOUR WIN. / YOUR STYLE. Terminal checks at 9.5–12.75; Pixel Arcade release at 12.75–16. Controlled hard cut; labels identify each. No rapid flashing montage.
- 16–19.5: SIX THEMES. / YOUR CALL. Genuine popup UI close-up with a cursor selecting Terminal. Simple caption: Silent celebrations. No account needed.
- 19.5–24: YOU SHIPPED IT. / TAKE A BOW. Footlit wordmark, free Chrome + Firefox installation, footlit-app.vercel.app. Perforated admission-ticket CTA lands on the beat; final frame holds.

## Visual identity
Paper #F1E8D4, ink #17120E, accent #F6B63B; League Gothic display, Newsreader editorial, DM Mono utility. Composition uses local brand fonts.
Foreground: cursor, badge, ticket. Middle: real product footage. Background: theatre rules and stepped light cones.

## Audio and licensing
Original score generated with the project's oscillator synthesis kit; no third-party recordings. 120 BPM, main hits 3, 4, 9.5, 12.75, 16, 19.5 seconds. Soundtrack belongs to the film; extension itself remains silent.
No audio-reactive extraction: the Python helper is unavailable on this machine. Motion follows the authored score's exact beat grid instead.

## Source integrity
Product overlays are the real src/engine and src/themes code, freshly rendered for this film. GitHub action context is a staged demonstration with fictional repository data. Popup capture uses the real built popup with a local browser-API fixture; no user data.
